CharaTalk Privacy Policy

Effective: July 27, 2026

CharaTalk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data. Period.

  • Everything runs on your device. The photos you import, the audio you use, and the videos CharaTalk generates are processed entirely on your Mac and are saved only where you choose to save them.
  • No network access. The app has no networking capability at all — it cannot upload, download, or phone home, even in the background.
  • No accounts, no tracking. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • App Store label. CharaTalk's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected," and this policy is the long form of exactly that.

If Apple's standard services (the App Store, TestFlight) collect data in connection with your download or beta participation, that collection is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours — we never receive it.

Questions: charatalk-support@grassroot-ai.com

Changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
